# STAYR — Case Study VSL Script Framework

**Target length:** 10–15 minutes · **Format:** presenter + screen walkthrough · **Viewer:** hotel owner, CEO, commercial / revenue / marketing director who has just opted in.

**Rules for whoever records this**

- No invented clients, numbers, testimonials or guarantees. Part 05 stays a placeholder until a real client has approved the data.
- Every claim about results needs a source, a date range and a measurement method on screen.
- Speak to a commercially literate viewer. No marketing-beginner explanations.
- One call to action only: book a growth strategy call.

## Part 01 — The Hook · 0:00–1:00

**Goal:** earn the next ten minutes by naming the gap between ad metrics and commercial outcomes.

**Talking points**

- Most hotel advertising reports look healthy. Clicks are up, ROAS looks strong — and the share of revenue arriving through commission-based channels hasn't moved.
- That's not a media problem. It's a measurement and system problem: the ad account is being managed, the acquisition economics are not.
- Promise of the video: how we connect creative, landing pages and campaigns into one system, and how we judge it on what a confirmed booking costs.

**On screen:** the six-stage journey — Ad Investment → Ad Click → Landing Page → Booking Engine → Confirmed Booking → Net Revenue.

---

## Part 02 — The Real Problem · 1:00–3:00

**Goal:** show we understand the viewer's P&L, not just their ad account.

**Talking points**

- **OTA dependency.** Commission is an acquisition cost. It buys the booking, but not the guest relationship.
- **Acquisition cost blindness.** Platform cost-per-conversion is recorded at the moment of booking — before cancellations, and without asking whether the guest would have booked anyway.
- **Conversion leaks.** Paid traffic sent to a generic homepage, then into a booking engine nobody has tested on mobile.
- **Disconnected systems.** Campaigns on a media calendar; pricing, restrictions and availability on another.

**On screen:** the "traditional agency thinking vs hospitality performance thinking" contrast from the homepage.

---

## Part 03 — The Opportunity · 3:00–4:30

**Goal:** reframe from "more bookings" to "more efficient acquisition".

**Talking points**

- Every stage between the click and the stay changes what a booking costs. Small improvements at several stages compound.
- Walk the illustrative model live: move one lever (landing page → booking engine rate) and show what happens to cost per acquired booking and net contribution. Say clearly that the numbers are illustrative.
- Reported ROAS is not incremental revenue. Explain why in one minute: attribution vs incrementality, and cancellations.

**On screen:** the interactive acquisition model from the homepage, screen-recorded.

---

## Part 04 — Our Methodology · 4:30–7:30

**Goal:** make the three engines concrete.

**Engine 01 — Creative (≈1 min)**
- One concept becomes formats, languages, placements and audience messages.
- How we brief, produce with AI assistance, review for brand and rate accuracy, then test.
- What a test is: a hypothesis, a variable, a decision rule.

**Engine 02 — Conversion (≈1 min)**
- Campaign-specific landing pages; the path from ad to booking engine.
- What we measure at each step, and what we can and can't see inside a third-party booking engine.

**Engine 03 — Paid Media (≈1 min)**
- Roles: Google captures existing demand, Meta creates demand and brings guests back, TikTok opens discovery.
- One budget logic across channels; daily management rather than monthly check-ins.

## Part 06 — Our Approach · 11:00–12:30

**Goal:** explain why this is different from a generalist agency.

**Talking points**

- **Specialisation.** Paid advertising only. No SEO, no social media management, no standalone branding.
- **Operators first.** Campaign decisions made with ADR, occupancy, booking windows and availability in view.
- **Daily execution.** Accounts are worked every day.
- **AI-assisted workflows.** AI speeds up production and experimentation. People decide what runs, what scales and what stops. AI does not guarantee results.

---

## Part 07 — The Partnership · 12:30–13:45

**Goal:** state the commercial model plainly so only qualified viewers book.

**Talking points**

- One service. 12-month partnerships only — and why: build, test across seasons, refine.
- **Annual:** €25,000 per year, paid upfront. **Monthly:** €2,500 per month on a 12-month agreement (€30,000).
- Advertising spend and applicable taxes are not included.
- Channels and deliverables are agreed during onboarding. Creative and landing page production is scoped, not unlimited.
- No guaranteed results. What we commit to is the work and the transparency.

---

## Part 08 — The Call to Action · 13:45–15:00

**Goal:** one clear next step for the right viewer.

**Talking points**

- Who should book: an established hospitality business with a meaningful advertising budget, a functional booking journey and the ambition to improve paid acquisition.
- What happens on the call: a look at the current setup, where the efficiency opportunities are likely to be, and an honest view on fit.
- "Book your growth strategy call below."

**On screen:** the booking block beneath the video.
